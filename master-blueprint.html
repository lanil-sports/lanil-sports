LANILSPORTS.COM

Master Operating Blueprint & System Specification

Louisiana High School Sports Directory, Recruiting Intelligence & NIL Valuation Engine ($0 Zero-Budget Stack)

1. Executive Vision & Market Opportunity

LANILSPORTS.COM (Louisiana NIL) is the definitive, centralized sports information, digital yearbook, and NIL intelligence platform for Louisiana high school student-athletes across all 64 parishes.

While national recruiting networks (On3, 247Sports, Rivals) cater almost exclusively to the top 1-2% of five-star recruits, LANIL owns the "98% Market Gap": the thousands of varsity student-athletes destined for D2, D3, NAIA, JUCO, or local commercial opportunities. LANIL combines athletic accomplishments, verified weekly statistics, recruiting milestones, and social media analytics into a unified public directory with subscription-based intelligence dossiers.

2. The $0 Cloud Architecture & Technical Stack

Edge Delivery & CDN

Cloudflare Pages ($0): Ultra-fast global static CDN, free SSL certificates, and custom domain mapping for lanilsports.com.

Database & Authentication

Supabase PostgreSQL ($0): Cloud database storing 64 Louisiana parishes, all LHSAA schools, and athlete profiles with Row-Level Security.

Automated Scraper / Cron

GitHub Actions ($0): Scheduled runners (2,000 free minutes/mo) waking up every Tuesday at 6 AM CST to parse Louisiana sports RSS feeds.

Monetization Gateway

Stripe ($0 Upfront): Zero monthly fee. Charges 2.9% + 30¢ only when parents or recruiters purchase subscriptions.

3. Complete 64 Louisiana Parishes Registry

LANIL covers every single one of Louisiana's 64 parishes: North Louisiana (Ouachita/Monroe, Caddo/Shreveport, Bossier, Lincoln/Ruston, etc.), Capital Area (East Baton Rouge, Livingston, Ascension), Greater New Orleans (Orleans, Jefferson, St. Bernard), Acadiana (Lafayette, Iberia, St. Landry), Central Louisiana (Rapides/Alexandria), Southwest Louisiana (Calcasieu/Lake Charles), and Florida Parishes (St. Tammany, Tangipahoa).

4. Dynamic NIL Valuation Mathematical Engine

Est_NIL_Value = [(Base + C_college) × M_stats × K_position] + S_social + B_multisport
  • C_college: SEC / Power 4 (+$1,500/offer) • Regional FBS (+$600/offer) • FCS/D2 (+$250/offer)
  • M_stats: Dynamic weekly multiplier (1.0x to 2.2x) scaling with cumulative touchdowns, passing yards, points, and strikeouts.
  • K_position: QB & Scoring PG (1.35x) • WR, Edge Rusher, Pitcher (1.15x) • RB, Center (1.0x) • Linemen (0.9x)
  • S_social: Instagram ($0.15) + TikTok ($0.08) + X ($0.10)
  • B_multisport: +$300 for multi-sport varsity athletes.

6. Anti-Scraping Defense & Legal Compliance

  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Public users cannot scrape sensitive guardian records or private recruiter notes.
  • Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode: Automatically blocks headless scrapers (Puppeteer, Scrapy, Selenium).
  • Anti-Framing Headers: X-Frame-Options: DENY blocks competitor iframe stealing.
  • Louisiana Act 810 (La. R.S. 17:3705): Minor parental consent verification, no school uniforms in commercial endorsements, zero gambling/alcohol sponsorships.
